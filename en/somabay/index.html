Frequently asked questions

How much do Somabay properties start from?

Prices start from EGP 15,900,000 on an 8-year installment plan with 5% down payment, for a 1-bedroom chalet in Blanca Gardens, Golf Town Marina Edition or Mazeej, with up to 30% off if you pay cash. Prices are based on the developer's price list as of October 2026.

Is there a cash discount in Somabay?

Yes. Up to 30% off the 8-year installment price, and 12.5% off the 5-year installment price. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll calculate the cash price for the unit you like.

What is the down payment and the longest installment plan?

5% down payment, with installments over 5 or 8 years. The unit price depends on the payment plan, and cash is the lowest price. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll send you the payment plan for the unit you like.

How far is Somabay from Hurghada Airport?

About 40 minutes by car from Hurghada International Airport.

Can I live in Somabay all year round?

Yes. It has schools (GEMS International School and the German Kompass school), a supermarket, pharmacy, medical center, gas station and restaurants, with sunshine all year.

What are the maintenance fees in Somabay?

Annual maintenance is about USD 10 per square meter, according to the developer.

What's the difference between Bayside and Reefside?

Bayside has calm water and a sandy beach close to the marina, golf and hotels, and suits families. Reefside has an open beach facing the sea and coral reefs, with wider sea views.

Is Homzegy the developer?

No. Homzegy is an independent real estate marketing platform. The developer is Somabay, and the contract is signed directly with them.
